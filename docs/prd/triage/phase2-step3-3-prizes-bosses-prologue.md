## Problem Statement

After Phase 2 step 3.2 (hypothesis H-6-1), the C generator replays VT's draws through `setMedallions` and stops. The next two stages in VT's order are `placeBosses` and `fillPrizes`. The C port has neither, and it also lacks the draw-free prologue that VT's `prepareWorld` runs before `setShops`, which `fillPrizes` depends on:

- **`placeBosses`** draws nothing in every case (`enemizer.bossShuffle` is always `none`), but it still writes the world: every dungeon's boss, the three Ganon's Tower slots, `Agahnim` in Hyrule Castle Tower and `Agahnim2` in Ganon's Tower. Under `mode.weapons` `swordless` it sets `Kholdstare` in Ice Palace first. The C world already starts with VT's constructor bosses, so this path changes nothing visible except Hyrule Castle Tower's boss. The step 2.2 note left one trap open: Ganon's Tower's `top` and `middle` slots (Moldorm and Lanmolas) cannot be told apart by any test yet. Only the spoiler's `Bosses` section names them.
- **`fillPrizes`** makes 64 draws in attempt 0 of every supported case. That is 14 for `randomCollection(15)` over the 14 `Prize` locations, 10 for `fy_shuffle` of the ten prizes, then 4 per prize candidate, because each candidate calls `getAdvancementItems()` again, which draws four random bottles. It places the ten crystals and pendants with an assumed-items access check and a win check after each placement. If a pass fails, it clears what that pass placed and starts again, up to 5 times. After that it throws `Cannot Place Prize`, which fails the attempt under the retry contract.
- **The prologue.** Before its pool getters, `prepareWorld` sets `accessibility` to `locations` for `completionist`, and places the `Triforce` at `Ganon` (or on the Master Sword Pedestal for `pedestal`). After the getters, it pre-collects `PegasusBoots` under glitched logics (and moves Boots out of the advancement list) and pre-collects `RescueZelda` outside `standard`. None of this draws. But `fillPrizes` calls `checkWinCondition` after each placement, and that check reads the `Triforce` (collected from `Ganon` or the pedestal) and the pre-collected items. Without the `Triforce`, every win check fails, every pass resets, and the attempt throws. The step 3.2 PRD left the prologue to this step (its H-5-9).
- **No check covers these outputs.** `stages.txt` has no `check` line for `placeBosses` or `fillPrizes`, and the C spoiler writer has no `Bosses` section (H-6-2).
- **The gate cannot afford them as it stands.** The plan's § Convergence gate caps `make -C tests/rando e2e` at 0.5 s. A step whose new claims or checks would break the cap must first restructure the gate, in its own commit, so that a claim's cost no longer grows with its number of due checks. At step 3.2's eight claims, e2e already takes about 0.26–0.29 s. The generator takes about 2 ms per case; the rest is `run_e2e.sh`'s separate awk passes over both `spoiler.json` files, two per due check plus two for the entries, about 25 ms per claim with checks (step 2.3.2 note). This step brings thirteen claims with four due checks each. At today's per-check cost, that comes to roughly 0.5–0.6 s.

The traps are in what the trace cannot see. Which prize goes to which location does not change the draw count. Popping from the wrong end of the shuffled list, or filtering crystal locations by the wrong class, keeps a perfect trace and still swaps every prize. `placeBosses` makes no draw at all. And crossWorld placement puts pendants in crystal dungeons (baseline: Misery Mire gets `PendantOfPower`), which a "crystals to crystal locations" reading would get wrong.

## Solution

0. **The gate first, in its own commit.** `run_e2e.sh` is restructured so that one claim costs a fixed number of passes whatever its number of due checks. Validation, verdicts and messages stay byte for byte as they are, and `make selftest` proves it with its expected outputs unchanged.
1. **The prologue, in the prepare module.** One draw-free function for the part before the getters: the completionist accessibility switch and the goal's `Triforce`. One for the part after: the Boots and `RescueZelda` pre-collects. The generator calls them where VT does, outside any stage, so an accidental draw trips the existing "draw outside any stage" assert.
2. **`placeBosses`' baseline path**, in the prepare module, with a new world setter for a region's boss slot.
3. **`fillPrizes`** in the prepare module, ported line for line: up to six passes, VT's location and prize orders, crossWorld placement, the assumed-items check, the win check, the reset of the pass's own locations, and a `false` return where VT throws `Cannot Place Prize`.
4. **Two supporting library functions.** The pool module gains a function that adds one getter list's items to a collection, drawing as the getter does (the four bottles). The world gains VT's `checkWinCondition`.
5. **The generator** runs stage `placeBosses`, then stage `fillPrizes`, after `setMedallions`. A `fillPrizes` failure fails the attempt (the first `return false` in `run_attempt`). Otherwise the generator stops with `RANDO_INCOMPLETE`.
6. **The spoiler writer** gains the `Bosses` section.
7. **The gate.** `stages.txt` gains `check placeBosses section Bosses` and `check fillPrizes keys *- Prize:1`. The eight claims of step 3.2 move up to `fillPrizes`, and five new claims cover the new code paths: `state-standard`, `goal-pedestal`, `goal-triforce-hunt`, `glitches-overworld_glitches`, `weapons-swordless`.

`make -C tests/rando e2e` then prints thirteen `PASS … (fillPrizes)` lines and 31 `PENDING`.

## Implementation Decisions

All decisions below are mine; no `guideline` agent was consulted. Each rests on the plan's Rules (including the claim ratchet and the e2e budget now in § Convergence gate), step notes 1.1–2.3.2, CLAUDE.md, the committed harness and library (at `7241144`, step 3.2 done), and VT's source at the pinned commit. Every assumption about step 3.2's outcome was a hypothesis labelled `H-6-n`; each has since been checked against the committed code (see Hypotheses), and the sections here apply the verdicts.

**Gate restructure (test harness, first commit)**
- Why now: the plan's budget rule (§ Convergence gate) and the measured cost (H-6-6 failed). Thirteen claims with four due checks each would exceed 0.5 s with the current per-check passes.
- What stays fixed: the command line, the environment overrides, the `claims.txt` and `stages.txt` formats, the exit codes, and the output (the `PASS`/`DIVERGENT`/`PENDING` lines, every detail line and the summary line). Due checks still run in table order and stop at the first failing one. Each failure prints exactly the lines it prints today (`<stage>: <kind> <arg>:` then `golden:`/`c:`, `c lacks …`), with `keys` entries compared in the same `LC_ALL=C` sorted order and `first_diff`'s `<end>` convention.
- What changes, the decided shape:
  - **Due checks per stage, once.** The due list (checks of rank ≤ the claimed stage, in table order) is computed once per distinct claimed stage, not once per claim.
  - **One pass per spoiler.** For a claim with due checks, a single awk program reads the golden `spoiler.json` and the C `spoiler.json` once each. It extracts every due `section` block and every entry line in the same run, evaluates the due checks in table order, and prints the detail lines of the first failing one, or nothing. The `pool` check, which reads `pool.txt`, folds into the same program when a stage owes it (none does yet). The cost per claim is then the generator plus a constant number of processes, whatever the number of due checks.
  - **The trace prefix in one process.** One awk replaces today's `awk` for the draw count, the two `head`s, `cmp` and `first_diff`, and prints the same messages.
  - **Claim validation in one pass.** The per-claim validation loop (unknown case, unknown stage, zero-draw stage, golden stage order) becomes one awk over `stages.txt`, `claims.txt` and the claimed cases' traces. It reports the first error with today's message, in today's order: claims in file order, and for each claim its checks in today's sequence.
- Proof: `make selftest` passes with its existing expectations unedited. Its fixtures already cover every trace message, every claims and stage-table error, `section` and `keys` failures (including `c lacks`, `<end>` and multi-word globs) and `pool`. The restructure may add fixtures, for example a claim with two due checks where only the second fails, to pin the table order and first-failure rule. A real-tree check backs it up: `make e2e` prints the same output before and after (8 pass, 36 pending). Under one temporary break of step 3.2's (swapping the medallion order), the `DIVERGENT` output is identical before and after.
- Time targets, measured on the same host as the median of five `time make -C tests/rando e2e` runs with the generator built: before ~0.26–0.29 s; after the restructure, at the same eight claims and two checks, ≤ 0.15 s; after this step's thirteen claims and four checks, ≤ 0.25 s. The plan's hard limit stays 0.5 s. If the restructured gate misses ≤ 0.15 s, the commit records the measured figures and what dominates, and the step continues only if the projected thirteen-claim time stays under 0.5 s.
- No other harness file changes: `cases.sh`, `selftest.sh`'s existing checks, goldens and the Makefile targets stay as they are.

**Facts read from VT, the goldens and committed code (not hypotheses)**
- `prepareWorld` order: completionist accessibility, the goal's `Triforce` (`pedestal` → `Master Sword Pedestal`; `ganon`, `fast_ganon`, `dungeons`, `ganonhunt`, `completionist` → `Ganon`), the four getters, the glitched Boots block (`logic` in MajorGlitches, HybridMajorGlitches, OverworldGlitches, NoLogic and `difficulty` not `custom`; `difficulty` is always `normal` here), `RescueZelda` when `mode.state` is not `standard`, then `setShops`, `setMedallions`, `placeBosses`, `fillPrizes`.
- `Triforce` and `RescueZelda` are `Item\Event`. The C item table has both as `RANDO_ITEM_CLASS_EVENT`, so the C location guard accepts the `Triforce` at `Ganon` (a `Prize\Event`). `Master Sword Pedestal` accepts any item except that it stores a Master Sword as `L2Sword`.
- The win condition (VT's `World` constructor): `has('Triforce')`, or North East Light World `canEnter` and goal `triforce-hunt` and `has('TriforcePiece', item.Goal.Required)`. `checkWinCondition` calls `collectItems` on its argument, which merges the pre-collected items again and skips locations already collected.
- `placeBosses` with `enemizer.bossShuffle` `none` (the oracle sets it, `oracle_case.php`): under `swordless`, Ice Palace's `Kholdstare` first. Then Eastern Armos Knights, Desert Lanmolas, Hera Moldorm, Palace of Darkness Helmasaur King, Swamp Arrghus, Skull Woods Mothula, Thieves Town Blind, Ice Palace Kholdstare, Misery Mire Vitreous, Turtle Rock Trinexx, Ganon's Tower bottom Armos Knights, middle Lanmolas, top Moldorm. Then Hyrule Castle Tower `Agahnim` and Ganon's Tower `Agahnim2` (its plain `boss` slot). VT's Hyrule Castle Tower constructor sets no boss, and the C table matches (`RANDO_BOSS_NONE`, `nboss_slots` 0). Every other value equals the C constructor defaults.
- `getSpoiler`'s `Bosses` reads `getBoss('')` for the ten dungeons and `bottom`, `middle`, `top` for Ganon's Tower. It writes `"Hyrule Castle": "Agahnim"`, `"Ganons Tower": "Agahnim 2"` and `"Ganon": "Ganon"` as literals. The golden block (baseline, identical in every supported case) has 16 keys in `strcmp` order.
- `fillPrizes`: `randomCollection(15)` over the 14 `Location\Prize` in `getLocations()` order. VT's `randomCollection` splices `get_random_int(0, count-1)` while locations remain, so it makes 14 draws. Then `fy_shuffle(array_diff(ten prizes, placed items))`, 10 draws. Each candidate calls `resetCollectedLocations`, `getDungeonPool()`, `getAdvancementItems()` (4 `getBottle` draws, `0..6`, or `0..5` without `rom.CatchableFairies`), `collectItems`, then the location's `canAccess`. A refused candidate goes back with `array_unshift`. `$attempts` starts at 5, and the recursive call is a tail call. A reset clears the phase's locations that were empty when the pass started. The four `isset` guards cannot fail once the candidate loop has run, and `$total_prizes == count` already catches an empty list.
- Simulating that reading on baseline's 24 non-bottle draws reproduces all ten golden prize lines (Turtle Rock Crystal2, Thieves' Town PendantOfWisdom, Ice Palace Crystal5, Skull Woods Crystal7, Misery Mire PendantOfPower, Palace of Darkness Crystal4, Swamp Palace PendantOfCourage, Tower of Hera Crystal3, Desert Palace Crystal1, Eastern Palace Crystal6).
- Goldens: every case's attempt 0 has exactly 64 `fillPrizes` draws (65 for `state-inverted`, unsupported): one `randomCollection`, one shuffle, ten candidates. So every prize location takes its first candidate, no pass resets and `Cannot Place Prize` never fires. Among the 42 supported cases, the prize entries take two values: baseline's, and the `crystals_*-random` cases'. `placeBosses` makes no draw in any case.
- The gate: a claim's stage must make a draw in attempt 0 (so `placeBosses` cannot be claimed, but its checks are due for any `fillPrizes` claim). `keys` globs match the 8-space entries of every top-level section except `playthrough`. The glob `*- Prize:1` matches exactly the ten dungeon prize keys.

**Prepare module (library, extended: `rando_prepare.h` and its source, H-6-1)**
- `rando_prepare_goal(RandoWorld *)`: the completionist switch and the goal's `Triforce`, each placement asserted. Called after world init, before the `getAdvancementItems` stage.
- `rando_prepare_pre_collect(RandoWorld *)`: `PegasusBoots` for the four glitched logics, `RescueZelda` outside `standard`, both with `rando_items_add` on the world's pre-collected collection. A `TODO` names step 2.3.6 for the Boots list edit. Called after the `getAdvancementItems` stage, before `setShops`. Neither prologue function takes a PRNG.
- `rando_place_bosses(RandoWorld *)`: the `none` path above, in VT's order, with the `swordless` line first. No PRNG parameter. Boss shuffle (`random`, `full`, `simple`) is enemizer, which the plan excludes: no config field, no branch.
- `bool rando_fill_prizes(RandoWorld *, RandoPrng *)`: returns false where VT throws `Cannot Place Prize`; the world is then in whatever state the last pass left, and the caller discards it.
  - Shape: a loop of at most six passes. Each pass draws the 14-location order (a local array of location ids, filled by splice), splits crystal and pendant ids in that order, builds and shuffles the remaining prizes (a local array of up to 10 item ids), runs the crystal phase, then the pendant phase. A phase returns "placed" or "failed". On "failed", it clears its own start-empty locations, and the loop starts the next pass or returns false.
  - Per candidate: `rando_world_reset_collected`, an empty `RandoItems`, the dungeon list then the advancement list added through the new pool function, then the prizes still to place, `rando_world_collect_items`, `rando_world_can_access`. On success, `rando_world_set_item` (asserted), then the win check with that candidate's collected collection.
  - Comments kept (CLAUDE.md, traps only): candidates pop from the end and a refused one goes back to the front; crossWorld sends pendants to crystal locations; the win check must not reset the collected locations; a reset clears only the phase's start-empty locations; every candidate makes its four bottle draws before the access check.

**Pool module (library, extended)**
- `void rando_pool_add_items(const RandoWorld *, RandoPrng *, RandoPoolList, RandoItems *)`: the same table walk and overrides as `rando_pool_build`, adding each item to the collection (`rando_items_add`, both views) instead of storing it. The same assert keeps random bottles in the advancement list. One shared internal walk serves both functions, so their item order and draws cannot drift apart.

**World (library, extended)**
- `void rando_world_set_boss(RandoWorld *, RandoRegionId, RandoBossSlot, RandoBossId)`: asserts the boss id is valid and that the slot is `boss`, or the region is Ganon's Tower. It does not use `nboss_slots`, which describes the constructor defaults the world dump checks (Hyrule Castle Tower stays at 0, so the step 2.2 fixture is unchanged).
- `bool rando_world_check_win_condition(RandoWorld *, const RandoItems *collected)`: `rando_world_collect_items` from `collected` into a local, then VT's condition, `Triforce` first (short-circuit order kept). It takes a mutable world because collecting marks locations.

**Generator (library, modified)**
- `run_attempt`, after world init: `rando_prepare_goal`. After the `getAdvancementItems` stage: `rando_prepare_pre_collect`. After `setMedallions` and the reset of the stage to `RANDO_STAGE_NONE` (H-6-1): stage `placeBosses` (`rando_place_bosses`), stage `fillPrizes` (`rando_fill_prizes` with the generator's PRNG; on false, reset the stage and return false), then `RANDO_INCOMPLETE`. The prologue runs with the stage at `RANDO_STAGE_NONE`.
- The comment on `run_attempt` ("Returns false when the attempt failed") already states the contract. This is its first false path. No golden takes it (step 1.2 note), and step 2.4's injection is what proves the loop. This step tests the false return at the unit level only.

**Spoiler writer (host, extended: `rando_spoiler_write_json` in `rando/host/`, header `rando_spoiler.h`, H-6-2)**
- A `Bosses` top-level object, always written, from the world's boss slots and the three literals, under VT's spoiler keys. The writer's key sort puts it first among the current sections. An empty slot asserts. The `TODO` for `Bosses` is removed.

**Gate files**
- `stages.txt` gains `check placeBosses section Bosses` and `check fillPrizes keys *- Prize:1`, after step 3.2's two lines (H-6-4).
- `claims.txt`: the eight claims of step 3.2 (`baseline`, `crystals_ganon-random`, `crystals_tower-random`, `item_functionality-hard`, `item_functionality-expert`, `item_pool-hard`, `item_pool-expert`, `item_pool-crowd_control`) move to `fillPrizes` (ratchet rule of step 3.2). New, each at `fillPrizes`:
  - `state-standard`: no `RescueZelda` pre-collect, and the Standard world's escape rules inside every `collectItems`.
  - `goal-pedestal`: the `Triforce` on the pedestal; every win check depends on reaching it.
  - `goal-triforce-hunt`: no `Triforce`; the win condition's second branch.

## Testing Decisions

- **A good test checks external behavior:** which item a location holds, which boss a slot holds, what the pre-collected collection holds, what `fillPrizes` returns, which draws an observer receives, and the text the writer produces. It never checks local arrays, pass counters or struct layout.
- **VT is the oracle.** Hand-written expectations restate VT's source or a golden's lines and cite them. A hand-written case that contradicts a golden is the test's bug (CLAUDE.md).
- **`test_pool` (extended):** for the advancement list on a baseline world, `rando_pool_add_items` adds the same multiset as `rando_pool_build`'s list from a twin PRNG with the same seed, with the same four draws. With `item_functionality` `hard`, the draws are `0..5`. The dungeon list makes no draw.
- **`test_world` (extended):**
  - `rando_world_set_boss` sets a dungeon's `boss` slot and Ganon's Tower's `top`.
  - Win condition: a collection holding the `Triforce` wins. Under `triforce-hunt` with `item_goal_required` 20, 20 pieces and North East Light World enterable win, 19 do not, and 20 pieces under goal `ganon` do not.
  - The collected-state trap: with the `Triforce` at `Ganon` and `rando_items_all` as the start, the check wins. After a `collect_items` that already collected `Ganon`, the same check with a start lacking the `Triforce` loses. It must not reset the collected locations.
- **`test_prepare` (extended; H-6-3):**
  - `rando_prepare_goal`: the `Triforce` at `Ganon` for the five goals, on the pedestal for `pedestal`. For `triforce-hunt`, `Ganon` keeps its constructor item and the pedestal stays empty. `completionist` sets `accessibility` to `locations`.
  - `rando_prepare_pre_collect`: `RescueZelda` for an open world, none for a standard world; `PegasusBoots` for each glitched logic, none under `NoGlitches`.
  - `rando_place_bosses`: every slot as the baseline golden's `Bosses` block, plus Hyrule Castle Tower `Agahnim` and Ganon's Tower `boss` `Agahnim2`. Under `swordless`, Ice Palace holds `Kholdstare`.
  - `rando_fill_prizes`, all-candidates-refused failure: with `item_count` overrides that make one crystal-class location unreachable (e.g. `CaneOfSomaria` 0 cuts Turtle Rock), it returns false after exactly six passes. An observer counts six draws of range `0..13`. The test first asserts its precondition through the public API: with the assumed collection, the location is not accessible.
  - Pendant-phase failure keeps the crystals: with overrides that cut one pendant-class location only (e.g. `BookOfMudora` and `MagicMirror` 0 for Desert Palace), it returns false, and every crystal-class location still holds a prize. The test first asserts its precondition: that location is unreachable, every crystal-class location is reachable, and the win check passes with the full assumed collection. If those overrides do not meet the precondition, the implementer picks others that do and records them.
  - The `Cannot Place Prize` assert does not exist: false is a value, so both failure paths are testable.
- **`test_generate` (updated, H-6-5):** baseline seed 1 gives `RANDO_INCOMPLETE` and exactly the golden's first 70 lines through the hook, the last 64 labelled `fillPrizes`. `crystals_ganon-random` gives the golden's first 71. The two-random-crystals order case gives 72 lines. After the baseline run, the world holds the golden's ten prizes. Step 3.2's 6/7-draw expectations are updated, not kept: they froze the old stop point (CLAUDE.md).
- **`test_spoiler_json` (extended; H-6-2):** on the world of a baseline generation stopped after `fillPrizes`, the `Bosses` block equals the baseline golden's block. The ten prize lines equal the golden's.
- **Driver and gate:** covered by the e2e claims and chunk 5's three breaks. The gate restructure (chunk 1) is proven by `make selftest` with its existing expectations unedited; new fixtures may only be added.
- **Speed:** `test` gains hand-written cases only. The two failure cases make about 1 800 draws and about 60 `collect_items` runs each, which is milliseconds on the host. Nothing moves into `logic`.
- **Prior art:** step 3.2's `test_prepare` for stage-level world checks; `test_generate` for driving a generation through the hook; `test_world` for collected-state and access checks; `test_pool` for twin-PRNG comparisons.

## Further Notes

- **Why the prologue is owed here.** `fillPrizes`' win check needs the `Triforce` (at `Ganon` or the pedestal) and the pre-collected items. Without the `Triforce`, every pass resets and the attempt fails, so no claim can pass. The completionist switch is ported with it so that the prologue lives in one place. Only the Boots list edit waits: it only affects lists nothing reads before step 2.3.6, and editing them now would break the pool-list replay against VT's raw getter output.
- **Why a false return, not an assert or a status code.** `Cannot Place Prize` is an ordinary attempt failure under the retry contract, like an unwinnable fill. `run_attempt` already returns false for "retry". A bool keeps the library free of error strings. The trace already shows where the failing attempt stopped, so no error string is needed to say why.
- **Why the trace is not enough.** The prize-to-location mapping is invisible to it. Any wrong pop end, class filter or crossWorld reading keeps all 64 draws and changes the `- Prize` entries. Hence the `keys` check, and the deliberate break that proves it.
- **Step note material** for `### Step 2.3.3`: the gate restructure and its e2e times (before, after at eight claims, after at thirteen), the claims and why the others are not claimed, the two `check` lines, `test` times, the three deliberate breaks and their messages, the prologue split (Boots list edit owed by 2.3.6), the unit-level-only proof of the false path, stack figures if measured, and where each hypothesis landed.
